How it works

Banks and NPCI run a voice line called 123PAY. You call it, it reads out a menu, and you press keys to pay. It works on any phone with no data, because it is only a call.

The hard part is pressing the right keys at the right moments while it talks. ZeroMB opens your dialer with those keys already typed in, with short pauses between them. You tap a pack, press the green call button, and type your UPI PIN when asked.

Set up once, with data on

  • Confirm your operator. The app picks it from your SIM. It never asks for your phone number.
  • Call 123PAY once from the app and say your bank name so it knows which account to use.
  • The language menu only plays on that first call. If it got set wrongly, the app has a Fix language button that presses # at the end of the menu, then 1, then the English key (3, Hindi is 2).

If a key is wrong on your line, every key and the waiting speed can be edited inside the app.

Packs it knows

The amount you pay is the pack. Pick one below in the app, or type any price. If the price is not a real pack for your operator, the recharge fails and the payment is refunded. Prices change, so check with your operator.

Is it safe

  • It asks for no permissions. It hands a phone number to your normal dialer, the same as tapping a number on a web page.
  • It stores your number, operator and packs on your phone only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • It never sees your UPI PIN. You type that into the call yourself.
  • It holds no money and is not a payment app. The payment is between your phone and NPCI.
  • Do not press keys during the call while the voice is talking. A key pressed at the wrong moment can pick the wrong option.
  • Some dialers ignore the pauses. If yours does, dial 080 4516 3666 by hand and use the keypad.
